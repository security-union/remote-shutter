Turn any two devices into a wireless remote camera system — no internet, no WiFi, no account needed. Just connect and shoot.

Remote Shutter uses peer-to-peer technology to link two devices directly. One becomes the camera, the other becomes your remote control with a live monitor. Perfect for group photos, selfies, tripod shots, creative angles, and hands-free photography.

FEATURES
- Wireless remote shutter from up to 50 feet away
- Live camera preview on your remote device
- Switch between front and rear cameras remotely
- Camera flash control
- Adjustable photo timer
- Record video remotely
- Works with iPhone, iPad, and Mac
- No internet or WiFi network required — direct device-to-device connection

HOW TO USE
1. Open Remote Shutter on two devices
2. Devices connect automatically via peer-to-peer
3. Choose which device is the camera and which is the remote
4. Start shooting from anywhere in the room!

Whether you're setting up a family photo, recording content, or capturing hard-to-reach angles, Remote Shutter gives you full wireless control of your camera from another device.
