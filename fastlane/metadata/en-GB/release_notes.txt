NEW — Multicam: up to four cameras, one director

- Connect up to four iPhones as cameras and see every angle live in a grid
- One synced shutter fires photos or video on every camera at the same instant
- Footage collects automatically to the director, time-aligned for editing

The remote is redesigned around the viewfinder: the picture first, the controls out of its way.

- Full-screen live preview: the shot now fills the display with the controls floating over it, instead of a small picture squeezed above a panel of buttons
- Landscape support: turn the remote sideways and the image takes over almost the entire screen
- A tidier layout: self-timer, aspect ratio and quality moved into a panel one tap away, each showing its current setting at a glance
- Clear connection status: a quiet indicator confirms the picture is live, and says so the moment the feed stalls
- Nothing covers your shot: capture progress now appears on the shutter button instead of a pop-up over the frame you are composing
- Camera standby: the device acting as the camera can rest its own screen during long shoots while it keeps streaming and shooting

Recently added:

- Stormo QUIC P2P engine: the device-to-device link rebuilt on QUIC, the transport behind HTTP/3, encrypted with TLS 1.3 and still direct device to device, with no router or network needed
- Please update Remote Shutter on both devices. Older versions can't pair with this one
- Tap to Focus: tap the live preview to set focus and exposure exactly where you want it, included with Pro
- Real-time video: a smooth live feed replaces still snapshots, so framing every shot is effortless
- Gear Store: browse tripods, mounts, and accessories picked for your camera setup
- Reliability and performance improvements throughout
