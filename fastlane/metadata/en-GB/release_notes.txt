A major upgrade to the connection itself: the new Stormo engine.

- Stormo QUIC P2P engine: the device-to-device link is rebuilt on QUIC, the transport behind HTTP/3
- Self-healing connection: pairing lands quicker, live preview holds steady, and dropouts heal themselves
- Encrypted with TLS 1.3 and still direct device to device, with no router or network needed
- Please update Remote Shutter on both devices. Older versions can't pair with this one

- Redesigned zoom slider: finer, smoother control from across the room
